# Hyimg design contract

The machine-readable contract is `design/contract.json`. This page says what it holds and how the checks use it. The values come from the code as of 2026-10-06 (`review/ui/look.css`, `slider.css`, `menu.js`, `icons.js`, the pages' `:root`, `DESIGN.md` and the owner's dated quotes in comments). Where the code disagreed with itself, the contract takes the majority or the value the owner stated, and the disagreement is listed in `contract.json` under `disagreements`.

## Icons and colours

«Одно значение, одна иконка, только из ui/icons.js; цвета только токенами» (owner 2026-10-07, after Image Studio's Adjustments tab wore the opacity's half circle).

- The registry is `review/ui/icons.js` (`HY_TOOL_IC`). Each glyph has one name, and the comment after it says what it means. A new meaning gets its own glyph there; a glyph that only looks close is not reused.
- Pages and plugins draw icons only through the registry: `hyIcon(name, size, line, cls)` in code, `<svg data-ic="name" width height stroke-width>` in static markup (filled in while the page is parsed), `hyIconPath(name)` on a canvas, and the `--hy-ic-*` images in CSS. Older names a page still passes sit in one table marked `hy-icon-names` (ui/menu.js `NAME`, Image Studio's `ICON_NAME`, 3D Studio's `FAMILY`).
- Icons are drawn in `currentColor`. Raw Editor's «on» RGB circles (`hyGradeIcon`) are the only icon with colours of its own.
- A drawing that is not an icon (the board's arrows, the marks drawn on a frame, the axes gizmo, a cursor) carries `hy-allow: icon-inline <what it is>` on its line, or sits between `hy-allow-begin: icon-inline <what it is>` and `hy-allow-end`.
- In CSS a colour is a token: a hex, `rgb()` or `hsl()` is written once, as a token's value (`--sel: #3b82f6`) or a `var()` fallback. The colours written before the rule are in the baseline (`css-color`); a new one fails.

## Primitives

Owner 2026-10-07, after the inventory (`docs/ui-inventory.md`): a control that has a primitive is built only from it. A hand-built switch, checkbox, key cap, badge or plate is a defect; a missing variant goes into the primitive.

- What exists, in `review/ui/hy/` (one file per element): `hy-button` (variant plain, solid, ghost, danger, reset; size s 24, m 28, l 30, row 32, dock 34, plate 38; pressed, toggle, disabled, icon, kbd), `hy-icon-button` (registry icon, xs 20 to plate 38, label, shape), `hy-switch` (30 × 18; variant well 44 × 26, the settings' look), `hy-check`, `hy-chip`, `hy-swatch` and `hy-swatches`, `hy-segmented` (on `ui/seg.js`, tabs variant, tint variant: no track, the chosen option tinted with the selection's blue, `--hy-tint-bg` and `--hy-tint-ink`; well variant: a dark well, the chosen option on a grey plate, `--hy-well` and `--hy-thumb`, owner 2026-10-08, round 11 of Settings), `hy-kbd` (20, 18, 15), `hy-badge`, `hy-hint`, `hy-info`, `hy-plate`, `hy-keyhint` (the keys of what the person is doing now, beside it; `window.hyKeyHint.show(anchor, ctx, items)`, three items at most, learned after 5 uses, `cv.keyhint` always, learn or off; owner 2026-10-08).
- The Hints family (owner 2026-10-08, DESIGN.md «Семья подсказок»): three members, picked by what the person is doing. The key hint (`hy-keyhint`, built) shows the keys of the action in progress beside it. The hint bar (specified, not built) is the same primitive shown with `place: "top"`: the main keys of the Studio or tool just opened, top centre on the `--hy-row-under` line, 26 px tall, a 1 × 12 px hair of `--line` between at most 4 items, gone after the first use of one, out of the way of a toast on the same line. The tip (`hy-tip`, specified, not built) is one line of 11 px in `--hy-hint` behind an 11 px bulb (`tip` in the registry), as long as its place holds (about 6 words in a header or a field, 12 on an empty state; cut with an ellipsis, its `title` always the whole text), gone from the search field once something is typed, the next one every 6 s (the old one fades and lifts 4 px in 0.3 s), still while pointed at, learned after one use (`cv.keyhintUsed`, key `tip.<ctx>.<id>`), bare on a block or in a field and on the key hint's glass over pictures. One setting, `cv.keyhint`, governs all three. The static look of the two new members is the block `spec:hints` at the end of `ui/hy/showcase.css`.
- Blocks (owner 2026-10-09, round 12 «A · Line», DESIGN.md «Блоки»): classes, not elements, in `ui/hy/block.css`. `.hy-blk` a block (`--hy-blk`, a `--hy-pline` hairline, 14 px corners); `.hy-bh` its 34 px header, `.hy-bh-tab` a 24 px tab with a 14 px icon, the chosen one on `--hy-tab-on` (ink at 8 %), `.hy-bh-n` a count, `.hy-bh-btn` a 24 px button; `.hy-sec` and `.hy-sh` a section and its 28 px head (12 / 500, `--sub`, no capitals), `.hy-sub` a 10 px label in capitals inside one; inside `.hy-blk` every control is a dark well 24 px tall with 6 px corners in both shapes (`--hy-ctl-h`, `--hy-ctl-r`; the slider, the search, `.hy-well`), a button on the block `.hy-wbtn`; a value is `--hy-val` (grey, a Studio's colour in a Studio). Room: `--hy-gap-row` 2, `--hy-gap-ctl` 4, `--hy-gap-sec` 6, `--hy-gap-blk` 8. `hy-segmented[variant=tabs]` wears the header's tabs; `ui/split.js` is the Line divider (a hairline, the accent `--hy-acc` 3 px under the pointer; `.hy-split-gap` between two blocks). Image Studio, 3D Studio and Dev Studio take these classes from the core.
- `<hy-avatar>` (owner 2026-10-07): a person's face, his picture or his initials on his colour (the initials drawn from `data-i` by CSS, so they are not words of the row), sizes 16, 20, 24, 32; an agent acting for him adds a round badge on the bottom right, 45 % of the face, ringed 2 px in `--hy-av-ring` (the panel by default), its glyph from the registry (`agentClaude`, `agentCodex`, `agentGemini`, `agentKimi`, `agentOpencode`, `agentAny`) on its kind's token (`--hy-ag-*`). Defined by `ui/avatar.js`, a classic script (Home loads no modules), which also gives `hyAvatarHTML(o)` for pages that write HTML strings and `HY_AGENTS.kind(name)`; the look is `ui/hy/avatar.css`. No company logos.
- How they are built: autonomous custom elements in the light DOM, state as attributes mirrored to ARIA, a real `<button>` or `<input>` inside, events prefixed `hy-`, each defined once per document. Their CSS reads tokens only, so `<hy-*>` markup is drawn right before the module upgrades it.
- Tokens: `review/ui/tokens.css`, the one palette (it was written four times), the heights `--hy-h-*`, the corners by shape (`--hy-row-r`, `--hy-plate-r`, `--hy-bar-r`, `--hy-btn-r`, `--hy-seg-r`, `--hy-chip-r`, `--hy-switch-r`). `data-hy-theme` and `data-hy-shape` on an element switch the tokens under it (the showcase puts the four looks side by side).
- Loading: `ui/tokens.css`, `ui/hy/hy.css` and the module `ui/hy/index.js`. Image Studio loads them itself (an iframe has its own registry); 3D Studio and Dev Studio run in the board's document.
- Types: JSDoc, `tsc -p jsconfig.json` over `review/ui/hy` (scripts/check.sh runs it when `tsc` is on the machine).
- Showcase: `review/ui/hy/showcase.html`, live on the Hyimg App board (page «UI», cards `html/hy-primitives/*.html`).

## Saving: revisions and merged edits

Owner 2026-10-08: two edits of one board (two windows, two Macs over Dropbox, an agent through `hy.py` while the owner works) never overwrite each other (`review/merge.py`, `ui/merge.js`).

- Every board file the server writes has `revision` (+1 per write), `vid` (random, names this version), `parent` (the `vid` it replaced), `edited` (epoch seconds of its newest change) and `by`. A save sends the board with the `revision` and `vid` it was loaded with, and the page adds `?edited=` (its last edit). There is no 409 for a stale save any more.
- Same base as the file: written as is. Otherwise a three-way merge with the base from the cache (`~/Library/Caches/Hyimg/<board>/bases/<page>/`, then History): by id, then by field (`x`+`y` is one field, `w`+`h` another), group members as sets, the newer change wins a field both sides changed, an edit beats a delete. A merged save answers with `merged {from, conflicts, base}` and the written `board`.
- What the person sees: a toast «Merged changes from {who}», plus «Changes to the same thing: the newer one is kept, the other is in History» when there was a conflict, kind `info`. Selection, camera and a drag stay. In History › Activity the event rows «Changes merged» and «Dropbox copy merged» list one line per field; History › Versions gets the losing side's board.
- A page save writes only `boards/<page>.json` (and the note files derived from it); comments, drawings and other pages' notes are separate files and never written by it.

## Menus

The board's right-click menu follows the kind of thing clicked (owner 2026-10-07, refining 2026-10-06's «grey, never hide»).

- An item that makes no sense for the kinds in the selection (a picture, a video, a PDF, a plugin's card, a note, a heading, a timeline, a group) is not shown. An item for those kinds that can't run right now is grey, its reason in the tooltip (`hyMenuOff`), skipped by the arrow keys. A mixed selection shows what fits at least one of its kinds. The order is the same for every kind.
- The links to the object and to the view, the image and the file path sit in «Copy as ›». The properties are three submenus, Copy, Paste and Clear, each listing only the kinds the selection can have.
- Links come in pairs, the app's first (owner 2026-10-07): «App link» (`hyimg://board/<board id>?page=…&obj=…`, icon `open`), «Browser link» (`http://127.0.0.1:<port>/?view=canvas&…`, icon `link`), then the same two for the view; the empty board shows the view's pair. Without a catalog id the app's item is grey with its reason. Built by `ui/applink.js`, parsed by `native/Links.swift`.
- A board opened in a normal browser shows one plate of the top row in its middle: `<hy-plate kind="capsule">` with «Open in Hyimg» (`<hy-button variant="solid" icon="open" size="l">`) and «Stay in browser» (ghost). Never inside the app, never with `?stay=1`; the setting «Open board links in the app» (`cv.applinks`, off by default) sends the page to the app on load.
- A plugin's `HY.ctx` items come only for the kinds the plugin knows; `off: "reason"` greys one within them (`HY.menuOff`).
- Measured on 2026-10-07 at a 1400 × 1000 window: a picture's menu 859 → 656 px, a PDF's 859 → 688, a note's 859 → 410, a group's 859 → 453, a 3D card's 859 → 442.

**Baseline.** `design/baseline.json` holds what is known today: `static` by rule, file and line text, `runtime` by page, check and key (no coordinates). A check fails only on something new. When a known violation is fixed, the count drops: `validate.py --update-baseline` writes it down, and the audit is rewritten with `HY_AUDIT_UPDATE=1`.

**Silencing one line.** Put `hy-allow: <rule> <reason>` in a comment on the line or the line above it.

## File size

Owner 2026-10-07: «чтобы файлы не содержали больше тысячи строк на один файл», «допустимо, если будет 1050 или 1100, это окей». The rule is `file-size` in `scripts/validate.py` (its code is `scripts/validate_size.py`), so `check.sh --fast` and the pre-commit hook run it on the four repositories.

- A source file is about 1000 lines and fails above 1100. Source means .py, .js, .mjs, .cjs, .ts, .html and .htm with their inline script and style, .css, .swift, .m, .mm, .h and .sh, tests and `native/` included.
- A line is about 160 characters and fails above 200. Without this the line count says little: `review/canvas.html` had lines of 884 characters.
- Not counted: data and generated files (json, md, svg, images, a file with `@generated` in its first 5 lines), vendored code (`vendor/`, `node_modules/`, `*.min.js`, `*.min.css`), builds and caches (`dist/`, `build/`, `__pycache__/`, `.venv/`, `_review/`, folders whose name starts with a dot). URLs do not count towards a line's length, and the lang tables (`lang.js`, `lang-*.js`) have no line limit.
- A file near the limit is split by responsibility, one concern per module, not cut into chunks of 1000 lines. The split is a change of its own with no change in behaviour, the full suites run before and after.

Files over the limits today are listed in `design/baseline.json` under `file_size` with their lines (when over 1100), their count of lines over 200 and their longest line. If any of the three grows, the check fails and names the file, its size and the limit, so these files can only shrink. `--update-baseline` lowers the numbers of a file that shrank and drops a file that is back under the limits. It never raises a number and never adds a file. Between 1000 and 1100 lines a new file only gets a warning in the summary.
